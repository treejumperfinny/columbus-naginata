01 — what is naginata

The practice

Your new favorite
martial art.

Atarashii Naginata (あたらしいなぎなた, literally "New Naginata") is a modern Japanese martial art centered around a traditional polearm. Developed after World War II to unify traditional battlefield styles (koryu) into a modern sport.

02 — about us

A place to begin

Come as
you are.

Columbus Naginata Club is a neighborhood dojo built around community. We believe that everyone deserves opportunities to grow and a safe space place to train. No experience needed.

Driven by one's unwavering commitment to the practice, we began with nothing more than a parking lot and a passion. Today, that spark has grown into a thriving community that has transformed the lives of countless students.
